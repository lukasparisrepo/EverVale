# 47 — Military Roster & Art Direction

Status: **CANONICAL DEFENSE CAST v0.1**

## Tone

EverVale defense is playful, chunky and readable.

It is **not** a realistic modern-war simulator.

Visual language:
- rounded armor plates
- compact oversized wheels/tracks
- chunky turrets
- visible suspension
- warm olive/cream/copper base
- restrained teal Varum glow at advanced tiers
- readable silhouettes
- slightly toy-like diorama proportions, but premium materials

Weapons should feel satisfying and fantastical rather than copied from real-world military hardware.

## Infantry

### Town Guard — T0
Role:
- basic ground defense
- patrol
- first response

Visual:
- coat + small chest armor
- compact stylized carbine
- lantern/radio-like device

### Shield Guard — T1
Role:
- holds chokepoints
- protects civilians/other guards

Visual:
- large rounded shield
- compact sidearm
- visible village insignia

### Rocket Ranger — T3
Role:
- long-range burst
- can help against light air

Visual:
- short chunky shoulder launcher
- oversized goggles
- colorful exhaust trail

## Ground vehicles

### Scout Buggy — T2 visual/support
Fast reconnaissance/service combat vehicle.

Visual:
- four large wheels
- roll cage
- small turret/searchlight
- lively suspension

### Armored Car — T2
Gameplay:
- mobile early heavy support

Visual:
- compact armored body
- chunky six wheels
- short cannon
- storage packs and flag

### Rocket Truck — T3
Gameplay:
- long-range volley
- ground + air support
- fragile if enemies reach it

Visual:
- small tracked/wheeled chassis
- six oversized stylized rocket tubes
- dramatic arcing colored trails
- reload animation

### Tank — T3
Gameplay:
- durable ground anchor

Visual:
- broad low body
- rounded turret
- intentionally compact barrel
- oversized tracks
- not based on a specific real tank

### Beam Tank — T5
Gameplay:
- late Varum anti-heavy / anti-air hybrid

Visual:
- ring-shaped emitter instead of ordinary cannon
- teal energy chamber
- floating/rotating focusing fins
- visible charging beam

## Static defense

### Watchtower — T1
- ground + light-air counter
- rotating compact turret/searchlight

### Heavy Turret — T3
- ground anti-heavy
- slow, punchy projectile

### Anti-Air Battery — T4
- fast tracking
- bright tracer/energy burst
- dedicated heavy-air counter

### Varum Shield — T5
- structure damage reduction
- spectacular translucent hex/ripple dome during attack

## Aircraft

### Skylark Fighter — T4
Gameplay:
- flexible air + ground support

Visual:
- short wings
- broad canopy
- oversized twin engines
- almost model-aircraft proportions
- contrails and tight turns

### Needlewing Interceptor — T4
Gameplay:
- dedicated fast anti-UFO

Visual:
- narrow swept silhouette
- forked tail
- teal nose sensor
- bright dash trail

### Cloudhammer — future/heavy
Gameplay:
- slow strike aircraft against large ground/UFO objectives

Visual:
- chunky twin-boom craft
- large underslung fictional payload
- no real-world bomber imitation

## Combat spectacle

The defense fantasy should be visually readable from management zoom.

Examples:
- Guards run from Guard Post.
- Watchtower rotates toward a buzzing UFO.
- Rocket Truck fires a curved volley.
- Tank shell creates a chunky dust burst.
- Fighter loops overhead.
- UFO beam bends street lamps and throws props.
- Varum Shield ripples when hit.

## Automation

The player does not drive vehicles or aim weapons.

Server decides:
- deployment
- valid target
- damage
- retreat/repair

Client presents:
- pathing
- turret tracking
- recoil
- projectile arcs
- explosions
- smoke
- shield impacts

## Repair

Vehicles are disabled rather than permanently destroyed in MVP.

Disabled state should look charmingly dramatic:
- smoke
- crooked hatch
- blinking warning light
- repair crew arrives later

This keeps combat exciting without making a cozy builder punitive.
