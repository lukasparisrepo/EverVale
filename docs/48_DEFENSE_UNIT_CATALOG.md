# 48 — Defense Unit Catalog

Status: **CANONICAL DEFENSE CONTENT v0.1**

## Art direction

EverVale defense is **stylized fantasy-tech**, not a realistic military simulator.

Design language:
- rounded armored forms
- warm metal + dark charcoal
- brass/copper mechanics
- small restrained teal Varum glow
- chunky readable silhouettes
- exaggerated wheels/turrets/wings
- playful but capable

Avoid:
- modern real-world national markings
- photoreal weapon replicas
- grim military camouflage
- realistic missile doctrine

The units defend a cozy world from strange monsters and UFOs.

Tier: T3

Role:

Visual:

Tier: T3

Role:

Visual:
- 6-wheel rover

## Missile Wagon
Tier: T4

Role:
- anti-air / anti-large UFO burst

Visual:
- large articulated vehicle

- radar dish
- glowing launch rails

This is a fictional game unit, not a replica of a real missile system.

# Combat presentation

Units are autonomous.

The player:
- builds
- positions
- chooses production
- sets readiness/priority

Units:
- deploy
- drive/fly
- select target
- fire
- retreat/return
- repair

# Fun factor

Combat should include:
- turret tracking
- chunky recoil
- bright but controlled projectile trails
- UFO shield pops
- monster knockback
- small explosions
- units returning damaged
- repair crews after the wave

The goal is a **miniature animated battle**, not a tactical military simulator.
