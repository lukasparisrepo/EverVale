# 42 — Anti-Idle Progression Constitution

Status: **CANONICAL**

## Principle

EverVale is autonomous, but **not an idle auto-win game**.

The world may continue when the player is absent.

The world may not make high-level strategic decisions on the player's behalf.

## What can progress offline automatically

- Money production
- Power/Water production and consumption
- Varum production
- Tech Point accumulation up to decision gates
- Population Growth up to Housing
- already-approved construction
- already-approved blocker clearance
- already-approved repairs
- citizen routines in aggregate
- local incidents
- bounded threat resolution

## What does NOT happen automatically

- no new building placement
- no automatic road expansion
- no automatic blocker clearance unless already queued
- no automatic Tech specialization choice
- no automatic expensive military purchases
- no automatic map-expansion decision
- no automatic redesign of failed layouts
- no infinite repair when Treasury/maintenance is insufficient

## Hard caps

Resources have storage caps.

Population has Housing cap.

Construction has queue capacity.

Tech has decision gates.

Varum has storage cap.

Military has production queues and explicit purchase decisions.

Therefore passive time approaches a **plateau**, not victory.

## Tech decision gates

Tech Points may accumulate beyond a threshold, but major progression tiers can require a player-confirmed milestone choice.

Example:
- enough Tech for T2
- player returns
- selects initial T2 specialization:
  - Utility
  - Civic
  - Defense

The tier can be earned while the choice remains pending.

Later points may continue accumulating only to a bounded overflow cap.

## Condition and incidents

Long operation creates wear and incidents.

Prepared towns absorb them.

Poorly designed towns:
- lose efficiency
- consume reserves
- accumulate repair backlog
- lose Happiness
- stop Population Growth

They do not automatically solve the cause.

## Threats

Offline major threats are capped and damage is bounded.

Repeated absence with weak defenses may:
- disable noncritical buildings
- consume Money
- create repair queues
- suppress Happiness and output

Absence must never be strictly superior to active planning.

## Offline yield taper

Long absences use diminishing effective productivity after major storage/maintenance thresholds are reached.

This is not a hidden punishment; it emerges from:
- full storage
- full Housing
- Tech decision gate
- maintenance backlog
- incidents
- workforce limits

Avoid arbitrary “offline income -80%” if systemic caps already solve the problem.

## Return value

A long absence should generate:
- some gains
- some completed queued work
- history
- incidents
- one or more meaningful player decisions

Desired feeling:

**“The town lived without me, but it still needs me as its planner.”**
