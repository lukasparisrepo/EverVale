# 28 — Parks & Social Life

## Core behavior

Parks are automated social destinations.

The player places the park. Citizens decide to use it automatically according to:
- free time
- Happiness
- distance
- weather
- available capacity

## Activities

Client may portray:
- sitting on benches
- walking
- children playing
- picnic
- reading
- jogging
- conversations
- dog walking
- feeding ducks

## Gameplay

Parks provide Recreation coverage and support Happiness.

They are not manually staffed.

## Dynamic occupancy

High Happiness + good weather:
- visibly busier park

Low Happiness / rain / threat:
- quieter park

Night:
- reduced use
- lighting changes
- some social activity remains

## Events

Parks and plazas may host:
- school fair
- market
- concert
- seasonal festival

These events can temporarily override normal ambient schedules.
