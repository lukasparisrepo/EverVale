# 52 — Story, Campaign & World Lore

Status: **CANONICAL NARRATIVE FRAME v0.1**

## Narrative principle

EverVale is first a living-world builder.

Story should:
- explain Varum
- explain escalating strange threats
- create chapter milestones
- give the player a long-term sense of progress

Story should **not** constantly interrupt building with dialogue.

Narrative is delivered through:
- short event cards
- environment changes
- radio/radar reports
- civic notices
- unusual sightings
- chapter milestones

No voiced protagonist is required.

---

# World premise

EverVale is a quiet frontier valley where small communities rebuild and expand using ordinary craft, machinery and a recently rediscovered mineral:

**Varum**

Varum is unusually energy-dense and interacts with certain machines, shields and strange atmospheric phenomena.

At first it appears to be simply valuable.

As the settlement mines and studies it, increasingly unusual creatures and aerial anomalies appear.

The game never needs a hard-science explanation.

Varum is intentionally a coherent fictional rule, not realistic geology.

# Campaign structure

## Prologue — A Quiet Vale

State:
- 12 residents
- small functioning settlement
- no Varum infrastructure

Story:
- establish town
- clear first blockers
- build School/Park/Workshop
- meet first harmless/weird creatures

Threat:
- Nibblers / Critters

Milestone:
- reach T1

---

## Chapter I — The Blue Stone

Player reaches first Varum deposit.

Events:
- miners report humming crystal
- instruments react
- strange lights appear at night

Threat:
- stronger creatures
- first odd scavengers

Milestone:
- construct Starroot Varum Mine
- accumulate first Varum
- defend one Varum-related nuisance wave

---

## Chapter II — Signals Above the Ridge

Radar/Weather instruments begin receiving impossible signals.

Events:
- Drone sighting
- brief sky flash
- animals react before a contact
- radio interference

Threat:
- light drones
- Jelly/Scout UFO type

Milestone:
- build Radar
- survive first mixed ground/air encounter

---

## Chapter III — Falling Lights

The attacks become intentional rather than accidental.

Events:
- Drop Pods
- walkers
- UFO Raiders
- strange marks near Varum infrastructure

Milestone:
- field heavy ground defense
- unlock Airfield/AA path
- protect critical town sectors through a multi-lane assault

---

## Chapter IV — The Varum War

The player now understands that Varum activity has drawn a remote alien/unknown force.

This is not an international geopolitical war simulation.

It is a playful frontier defense conflict against bizarre machines/creatures.

Threat:
- carriers
- command saucers
- shield drones
- heavy walkers

Milestone:
- layered ground + air defense
- advanced Tech
- build Aurora Ward prerequisites

---

## Chapter V — The Sky Breaks

Late campaign spectacle.

A large broken alien structure / Mothership Fragment enters the valley.

It is not a planet-destroying apocalypse.

It creates:
- gravity anomalies
- mixed waves
- drone deployment
- several combat phases

Campaign victory condition:

1. Tech T5 reached
2. Aurora Ward operational
3. final story assault triggered
4. Mothership Fragment / command force repelled
5. Hearth Hall remains recoverable

On victory:
- story chapter completes
- cosmetic monument / civic celebration unlocks
- endless living-world mode continues
- threats remain but campaign is considered won

The world does not end.

# Game loss

There is no permanent save deletion.

A normal lost wave is a **BREACH**, not a global game over.

A severe collapse can enter:

**RECOVERY STATE**

Triggered when:
- Hearth Hall disabled
- Power/Water unavailable
- no combat-ready defenders
- critical repair backlog exceeds threshold

Recovery State:
- Population Growth stops
- nonessential production pauses
- emergency repairs receive priority
- threat intensity is temporarily reduced
- player rebuilds

The player can always recover.

This preserves cozy persistence while still making bad defense meaningful.

# Neighbor powers

Neighboring human states may exist in future content as:
- trade
- diplomacy
- story events
- optional conflicts

They are not required for v0.1 campaign.

Primary canonical combat identity is:
- strange creatures
- raiders/scavengers
- UFO/alien technology

This keeps the tone distinctive.
