# 02 — World & Art Direction

## Art target

**Premium cozy miniature + stylized realism.**

Forms are simplified enough to read from isometric distance, but materials, lighting, animation, and environmental storytelling give the world depth.

Avoid:
- toy-plastic uniform materials
- empty flat terrain
- excessive photoreal texture noise
- identical modular buildings
- mobile-game neon overload

## Camera-scale art rule

Every asset must work at two scales.

### Management zoom
Strong silhouette, readable category, visible operational state.

### Diorama zoom
Doors, windows, signs, props, vegetation, workers, pipes, cables, vehicles, wear, and animation reward inspection.

## Palette

Base:
- warm stone
- muted greens
- timber brown
- cream plaster
- copper/bronze accents
- charcoal industrial metal

Technology:
- Varum teal/azure glow used sparingly

Threat:
- readable warm danger accents without turning the world grimdark

## Materials

PBR-inspired but stylized:
- stone: rough, chipped, moss/dirt breakup
- wood: clear grain direction and worn edges
- metal: controlled highlights, not mirror-like
- glass: restrained reflections
- water: readable, soft, performant
- Varum: embedded emissive mineral, not flat neon

## Environment

Terrain should use height, water, vegetation, rock clusters, paths, civic spaces, and sightline framing.

The world must look authored even when generated procedurally.

## Day/night

Daily rhythm is visible:
- morning commuting
- daytime production
- evening recreation
- night lighting and patrol behavior

Night must remain readable.

## Weather

Weather may modify:
- ambience
- citizen choices
- energy demand
- visibility
- farming/output later

Weather should enrich cozy atmosphere before becoming a heavy simulation burden.
